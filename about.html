    David Ferraro

    About

    David Ferraro is a Colombian fashion and advertising photographer based in New York City.

    9+

    Colombian fashion photographer to shoot a magazine cover at Nevado del Cocuy

    With over nine years of experience across five countries, his work combines elegance, luxury and a distinct Latin sensibility with an international visual language.

    Ferraro approaches every campaign with a creative director’s mindset. His style pairs elegance and luxury with a strong Latin influence, positioning him within international fashion photography and advertising campaigns.

    He graduated with honors in Digital Imaging from LCI Bogotá and later completed an International Master’s Degree in Fashion and Advertising Photography at Too Many Flash in Madrid, Spain.

    His cover story for Harper’s Bazaar Vietnam, photographed at Nevado del Cocuy in Colombia, made him the first and only Colombian fashion photographer to shoot a magazine cover at this location.

    Ferraro is part of the global directory of Models.com, one of the most selective platforms in the fashion industry. He is currently based in New York and available for projects worldwide.
